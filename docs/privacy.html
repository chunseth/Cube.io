Cube.io privacy

Your practice.
Your device.

Last updated October 25, 2026.

Data stored by the app

Cube.io stores solve times, scrambles, sessions, training attempts, learned cases, custom algorithm content, and preferences locally on your device so it can provide timing, training, and progress features.

Accounts and collection

Cube.io does not require an account. We do not operate a server that receives your solve history or training progress. The app does not include advertising or third-party analytics services.

Backups and deletion

Device-level backups are controlled by your Apple settings. Cube.io does not provide its own cloud sync or full solve-history backup service. Deleting the app can remove its local data. Content exported through the app remains wherever you choose to save or share it.

Support and external links

If you contact us through GitHub Issues, the information you post is public and is used to respond to your request. External algorithm references, this website, and the support issue tracker are provided through third-party websites. GitHub’s services are covered by the GitHub privacy statement.

This website

This is a static site hosted by GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics scripts, advertising trackers, or a contact form.

Questions

For privacy questions, use the support page. Please avoid putting private information in a public issue.
