A cube timer and trainer
for iPhone and iPad.

Cube.io is for timing 3×3 solves and practicing OLL and PLL. It works offline, with your times and learned cases saved on your device.

What’s in the app

  • A solve timer with scrambles, optional inspection, penalties, and separate sessions.
  • All 57 OLL and 21 PLL cases, with algorithms and animated move previews.
  • Execution practice and recognition quizzes for the cases you’re learning.
  • Personal bests and Mo3, Ao5, Ao12, and Ao100 statistics.

No account is needed. For questions or bug reports, visit support.
